What BEach does

BEach contains setup and maintenance scripts for Jibo. They prepare community software and point the robot at BEefy for conversations and BEaker for updates. 5x1 serves these scripts at scripts.5x1.com.

A bridge into the ecosystem

The post-mod setup brings the robot-side software and hosted services together. The guide explains when to use the scripts and walks through the process on your robot.
