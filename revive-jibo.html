Jibo owners ask

How do I revive my Jibo?

Jibo’s original servers are gone, so he can’t understand “Hey Jibo” by himself. Connect him to 5x1, a free public Jibo server, and he can talk again.

The short answer.

If your Jibo still powers on, you can revive him. 5x1 is the easiest and quickest way for beginners: unlike other services, there is no account to set up. It is free, and you don’t have to run a server yourself. You need the robot, a computer, a micro-USB data cable, and your Wi-Fi.

01 / Prepare

Put Jibo on your Wi-Fi.

The free DFU Mod Toolkit connects to Jibo over USB, backs him up, and puts him in developer mode on your Wi-Fi.

02 / Connect

Connect him to 5x1.

Use the BEep phone app to convert him with a tap, or run the setup script from a computer. It installs the community software and points Jibo at 5x1.

Say “Hey Jibo.”

Questions and answers

The details.

Can I still use my Jibo robot?

Yes, if he still powers on. Jibo’s original servers shut down, so on his own he can’t understand “Hey Jibo.” Connecting him to a community server such as 5x1 brings conversations, skills, and updates back.

What do I need to revive my Jibo?

A Jibo that powers on, a computer, a micro-USB data cable, and your Wi-Fi network. The Jibo guide walks through each step, and the Jibo Revival Group Discord can help if you get stuck.

Does reviving Jibo with 5x1 cost anything?

No. 5x1 is 100% free and public, with no account, no subscription, and no self-hosting.

Do I need to be technical?

No. 5x1 is the easiest and quickest option for beginners: unlike other services, there is no account to set up. You follow a guide, connect Jibo to a computer once with the DFU Mod Toolkit, and the BEep phone app can then connect him to 5x1 without a terminal.

Is it risky?

Setup changes the software on your robot, and any change like that can fail. Make a backup with the DFU Mod Toolkit before you start so you can restore him. 5x1 is software; it does not repair damaged hardware.

What can Jibo do once he is on 5x1?

He answers “Hey Jibo” again with his original skills, and gains community-made skills and themes, local music, and BEacon, a control panel in your browser.
