Why choose 5x1?

Original Jibo spirit. Privacy first.

5x1 is the best fit for owners who want to stay true to the original Jibo behavior and style while making privacy a priority. The focus is on keeping the familiar personality, interactions, and look you love, with room to make Jibo your own.

Our privacy commitments are simple: no usage telemetry, no website tracking scripts, and no selling your data. Hosted conversations still send requests to a server; privacy-first doesn’t mean everything runs offline.

BEam builds on Jibo’s original system skills, carrying familiar experiences forward alongside custom themes and community additions. Keep the Jibo you love, with more ways to make him your own.

Data, in plain language

Know what you’re sharing.

A connected robot needs some data.
You should know what that means.

We collect only the information required to provide the features you use—nothing more. Jibo sends information to the hosted services to answer requests, connect to the right robot, and deliver software updates. Here’s what that involves, and what we don’t collect.

What the services use

Data that makes Jibo work.

  • Speech requestsAudio and transcripts sent for a conversation are processed so BEefy can understand and answer your request. Speech data is not stored by 5x1.
  • Robot and setup informationDevice identifiers, software versions, and service settings help connect your robot and provide the right updates. Profile information you provide supports the features you use.
  • Connection informationRequests expose connection details, including your IP address, to the service and its hosting provider. Connection data is not stored by 5x1.

What we don’t collect

Your robot isn’t a tracking tool.

  • No usage telemetryThe hosted service does not collect usage telemetry to track how you use your robot.
  • No website tracking scriptsThis website has no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. Its feature icons and other assets are hosted locally.
  • No cloud camera streamThe local camera-streaming feature sends video over your network, not through the BEefy conversation server.

Your data is never sold.

Free access does not mean your data is the price. We never sell your data.

A few things to know

Before your next “Hey Jibo.”

Will I need to pay later?

No. The hosted BEcosystem is 100% free forever. There is no subscription or paid feature tier.

Does free mean you sell my data?

No. We never sell your data. The services process information needed to connect your robot and answer its requests, without collecting usage telemetry.

Can I look at the code?

Yes. All five projects have public source repositories. Use the links above or visit the community and resources page to explore them.
