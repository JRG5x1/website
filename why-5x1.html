Choosing a Jibo server.

Other servers offer different combinations of setup, features, and data practices. 5x1 builds on Jibo’s original system skills, adds community features, and needs no account for ordinary use. Compare each service’s current features and privacy policy. Hosted 5x1 conversations still send requests to a server.

A fair side-by-side

Compare the things that matter.

5x1 is free to use and public source; other server projects may be free and open source too. Compare on experience and fit, not price alone.

Setup & accounts.

5x1 setup uses the DFU toolkit to set int-developer mode, then post-mod setup over SSH. Ordinary use needs no account. Other servers may use a different setup path or require an account; check what changes on Jibo and how recovery works before starting.

The Jibo experience.

BEam builds on Jibo’s original system skills and adds community skills, custom themes, local music, and BEacon’s local browser control panel.

Other servers may offer a different mix of original household features, apps, and tools. Check what’s available today—not only what appears in a catalog.

Data & privacy.

5x1’s published commitments: speech and connection data are not stored by 5x1; no usage telemetry and no selling your data. Other services may handle collection, retention, providers, and deletion differently; compare their published policies where available.

Hosted 5x1 conversations still send requests to a server, and external providers have their own practices. Read the data-handling section for details.

A little more detail

Know what you’re choosing.

What makes 5x1 a good fit for my Jibo?

5x1 is built for owners who want familiar Jibo behavior and style, privacy-conscious hosted services, and community customization. BEam builds on Jibo’s original system skills, with custom themes, local music, and a local browser control panel alongside familiar experiences.

Does privacy first mean everything runs offline?

No. Hosted conversations send requests to a server. Our privacy commitments describe how those requests are handled: 5x1 does not store speech or connection data, collect usage telemetry, or sell your data. This website has no tracking scripts. Read the data-handling section for the full explanation.

How does 5x1 compare with other servers?

Ordinary 5x1 use needs no account. BEam builds on Jibo’s original system skills with community additions, and 5x1 publishes commitments about speech, connection data, and telemetry. Other servers may use different setup paths, features, and data practices. Compare each service’s current setup and privacy policy. Hosted 5x1 conversations still send requests to a server.
