BEacon Jibo dashboard.

A clean, polished web UI for managing your Jibo from a browser on your local network.

More control than any other Jibo dashboard.

BEacon brings everyday robot controls together in one place. See Jibo’s screen, manage music, personalize his appearance, manage community skills, and keep software up to date.

Your Jibo, at a glance.

Open BEacon in a browser on the same local network as Jibo. View the robot’s screen, manage music, change his eye and theme, work with installed skills, and apply updates without juggling separate tools.

Make the experience yours.

Use the BEam skill collection and the community Store to explore themes and skills. BEacon is the control surface for bringing those choices to your robot.

No 5x1 account system.

5x1 has no user-account system, so BEacon does not require a 5x1 login or account pairing. GitHub authorization for some Store installs and pairing for optional integrations are separate from 5x1 accounts.

A few quick answers

Using BEacon.

What can I manage with BEacon?

BEacon lets you view Jibo’s screen, manage music, change his eye and theme, install and manage community skills, and apply updates from a browser on your local network.

Does BEacon require an account?

No. 5x1 has no account system, so ordinary BEacon access needs no sign-up or account pairing. GitHub authorization for some Store installs and pairing for optional integrations are separate.

Can I use BEacon remotely?

BEacon is designed for local-network access. Access from outside your home depends on your own network and security setup; 5x1 does not promise remote access automatically.
