BEaker

The update service delivering community skills and robot services to Jibo.

What BEaker does

BEaker is the public update service for the BEcosystem. It provides the update catalog and packages for BEam and BEnch, bringing robot skills and operating-system services to the same update path.

Keeping the pieces together

Your robot’s update client talks to BEaker at joap.5x1.com. The setup tools configure that connection, so you can use the robot’s own update interface after setup.
