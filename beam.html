BEam

On-robot skills, custom themes, and community experiences for Jibo.

What BEam does

BEam is the collection of on-robot skills and interface experiences in the BEcosystem. Built on Jibo’s original system skills, it carries familiar personality forward alongside community additions.

Custom themes and community skills

Customize Jibo’s eye, menu style, and icons with Custom Themes. The collection also includes a local-music jukebox, cooking videos, and Bad Apple. BEacon, included with BEam, adds a local web control panel for viewing the screen, managing music, changing the eye, and updating skills.
