BEam

The experiences you see, hear, and touch.

What BEam does

BEam is the collection of on-robot skills and interface experiences in the BEcosystem. Built on Jibo’s original system skills, it carries familiar personality forward alongside community additions.

More to explore on the robot

The collection includes a local-music jukebox, cooking videos, and Bad Apple. BEacon, included with BEam, adds a local web control panel for viewing the screen, managing music, changing the eye, and updating skills.
