BEefy

The conversation server Jibo connects to.

What BEefy does

BEefy handles the “Hey Jibo” side of the ecosystem: receiving speech, understanding requests, and sending responses back to your robot. When a request belongs to an on-robot skill, it hands the experience over to BEam.

A conversation starts here

Jibo connects to the hosted conversation service at api.5x1.com. The familiar robot interface stays on Jibo; the server handles the conversation behind it.
