Is 5x1 safe?

5x1 is open-source community software run by volunteers. Here is what it changes on your robot, how to undo it, and what it does not promise.

The short answer.

5x1 is free and open source, has no account system, and does not sell your data. It is a volunteer community project, not a company, and it has not had an independent security audit. Back up your robot before setup and keep him on a home network you trust.

Questions and answers

The details.

Is 5x1 trustworthy?

5x1 is built in the open by the Jibo Revival Group. The source code is public, the service is free, there is no account system, and 5x1 does not sell your data. It is run by volunteers and has not had an independent security audit.

What does 5x1 setup change on my Jibo?

Setup puts Jibo in developer mode on your Wi-Fi, turns on remote login (SSH) on your network, installs the BEam and BEnch community software, and points Jibo at 5x1 for conversations and updates.

Can I undo it?

Yes. The DFU Mod Toolkit can reinstall Jibo’s software or restore a full backup. Make that backup before you start.

Can setup break my Jibo?

Any change to a robot’s system software can fail and leave him needing recovery. That is why the guide starts with a backup. 5x1 cannot fix worn or damaged hardware.

Should I take any precautions?

Keep Jibo on a home network you trust, because setup turns on developer mode and remote login. Do not make him reachable from the internet, and do not share his address or passwords in public channels.

What happens if 5x1 goes away?

We have no plans to shut down the public server and intend to keep it running for the foreseeable future. This is our current intention, not a guarantee. The server is open source, so it can also be run by someone else.
