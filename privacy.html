Data, in plain language

Privacy policy.

Only what’s required. Clear about what that means.

In effect upon publication. Last updated October 26, 2026.

Your data isn’t the price.

We collect only the information required to provide the features you use—nothing more. We never sell your data. We do not collect usage telemetry, and this website has no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies.

Connected features still need to process information. Privacy first is not a promise that nothing leaves your home.

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes the 5x1 website and the hosted BEcosystem services operated by 5x1 for Jibo. It explains our commitments about information those services receive. Software you run yourself, optional integrations, and websites you visit through external links may handle information differently.

2. Information needed to make Jibo work

  • Speech requests. Audio and transcripts sent for hosted conversations are processed so BEefy can understand and answer the request. Speech data is not stored by 5x1.
  • Robot and setup information. Device identifiers, software versions, and service settings help connect the correct robot and deliver compatible updates. Profile information you choose to provide supports the features you use.
  • Connection information. Network requests expose technical details, including an IP address, to the receiving service and its hosting infrastructure. Connection data is not stored by 5x1.
  • Information you send for help. If you contact us, we receive whatever you include in that message. Please share only what is needed to explain the problem.

We do not collect usage telemetry to track how you use your robot. This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies, and its icons and other page assets are hosted locally.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request: understanding and answering conversations, connecting your robot, applying its settings, and delivering software updates. Information you send for support or a privacy request is used to handle that request. We do not collect extra information for advertising or sell your data.

4. Processing and retention

Processing a request is different from keeping a record of it. Our commitment is that speech and connection data are not stored by 5x1. Fixed retention periods for robot settings, profile information, and support or privacy correspondence have not yet been established; this policy does not promise automatic deletion after a specific interval.

You can ask what information is associated with your robot, how it is retained, or request its removal using the privacy contact below. Removing information required by a feature may stop that feature from working. We will explain any limitations that apply to a request rather than promise that information we do not control has been erased.

5. Hosting and optional services

5x1 uses hosting providers and feature providers to deliver its services. These providers are part of the service’s data path, not simply external links. Depending on the feature and configuration, services used include:

  • AWS. Hosting infrastructure used by 5x1, which processes network requests and associated technical information on the service’s behalf.
  • OpenRouter. AI requests may be sent through OpenRouter to the model provider selected by the configuration. The request content is processed outside the robot.
  • Ollama (local). A locally configured Ollama instance can process AI requests on the machine running it. Its location, access, and retention depend on that installation; this does not make every other Jibo feature offline.
  • Wolfram Alpha, News API, and weather services. Relevant questions, searches, or location information may be processed to provide the answer or content you request.

The providers involved depend on the features you use and their configuration; this list is not a claim that every request goes to every provider. Ask us which services are involved in a particular feature if you are unsure.

Our statements about data not being stored by 5x1 describe 5x1’s handling, not a guarantee that an external provider keeps no records. Hosting infrastructure and external request providers can have their own logging and retention practices. We have not specified provider-by-provider retention periods here. Contact us for details before sending sensitive information. Independently enabled optional integrations and services you operate yourself also have their own settings and policies.

Links to community, source-code, and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Information you post or send there is also handled by the platform you use.

6. Local features and shared robots

The local camera-streaming feature sends video over your network, not through the BEefy conversation server. Local controls and files remain subject to your device settings, network access, and any integrations you enable. Local operation should not be confused with a guarantee of secure access.

Jibo may be used by several people in a shared space. Let other users know when connected features are in use, and avoid including sensitive information in requests. A parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information can contact us through the route below.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose which optional integrations to enable, limit information you provide, or stop using hosted features. You can ask what information 5x1 holds about your robot, request a correction or deletion, or raise a concern. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

We may need enough information to confirm that a request relates to your robot before changing or removing its information. Do not send passwords, API keys, or other credentials. No online service or network can guarantee complete security.

8. Privacy contact

For a privacy question, correction, or deletion request, email contact@5x1.com. Start with a description of your request; we can arrange any additional information needed to identify the affected robot privately. Do not post recordings, device credentials, or other personal information in public channels, and never send passwords or API keys.

9. Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page. Review the policy when choosing or changing connected features, and contact us if anything is unclear.
