Jibo owners ask

What does 5x1 store?

A short version of the privacy policy: what leaves your home when you say “Hey Jibo,” and what 5x1 commits to.

The short answer.

5x1 has no accounts and collects no usage telemetry. Our privacy policy commits that speech and connection data are not stored by 5x1, and that we never sell your data.

Hosted conversations still send requests to a server. Privacy first is not a promise that nothing leaves your home.

Questions and answers

The details.

Does 5x1 keep recordings of what I say to Jibo?

Audio and transcripts sent for hosted conversations are processed so the server can understand and answer the request. Our privacy policy commits that speech data is not stored by 5x1.

Do I need an account?

No. 5x1 has no user-account system, sign-up, or account-pairing step. GitHub authorization for some Store installs and pairing for optional integrations are separate.

What information does 5x1 need to make Jibo work?

Device identifiers, software versions, and service settings help connect the correct robot and deliver compatible updates. Profile information you choose to provide supports the features you use.

Does anything go to other companies?

Some features use outside providers, such as hosting, AI requests, and weather or news answers. They can have their own logging and retention practices. The privacy policy lists them.

Does the 5x1 website track me?

No. This website has no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies.

Can I ask what 5x1 holds about my robot, or have it removed?

Yes. Email contact@5x1.com. Describe your request first; do not send passwords, API keys, or recordings.
