BEnch

JiboOS services that support the robot underneath its skill layer.

What BEnch does

BEnch contains the non-skill side of JiboOS: the operating-system services that support the robot. It sits beneath BEam’s interface and experiences as part of the robot-side software.

Two layers, one robot

BEam supplies the skill layer; BEnch supplies the services underneath it. BEaker distributes their updates, while the setup tools connect your robot to that update service.
